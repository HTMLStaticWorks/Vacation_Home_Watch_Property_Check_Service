Coastal Care Considerations

Coastal vacation properties require focused oversight due to salt-laden sea air, heavy summer humidity, rapid afternoon thunderstorm development, and high seasonal vacancy rates.

What Our Haven Bay Team Focuses On:

Exterior Fixture Corrosion

Observing outdoor lighting, hose bibbs, and shutter hardware for aggressive salt air corrosion.

Humidity & AC Airflow

Verifying thermostat humidity settings to discourage indoor mold development during peak heat.

Patio Furniture Readiness

Securing lightweight deck chairs, fire pits, and awnings prior to sudden tropical squalls.

Pool & Lanai Screens

Visual observation of pool cages, screen tears, pump noise, and water skimmer level.

Recommended Visit Schedules

For ocean-adjacent homes, we recommend weekly visits from June through November (hurricane and storm season) and bi-weekly checks during the winter months.
