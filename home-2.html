The Philosophy of
Proactive Stewardship.

Our role is not to replace emergency services or professional contractors, but to serve as your reliable local eyes—confirming that windows are secured, packages are cleared, and visual conditions match expectations.

Structured Flow

The Property Check Journey

How our specialists systematically review your property upon arrival.

PHASE 01
Arrival & Perimeter

We check driveways, mailboxes, exterior lighting, landscaping hazards, and signs of forced entry.

PHASE 02
Secure Entry

Disarming security access if requested, checking door alignment, weatherstripping, and window latches.

PHASE 03
Interior Walkthrough

Scanning ceilings, checking under sink cabinets, observing thermostat temperatures and breaker panels.

PHASE 04
Mail & Departure

Bringing in mail/packages, re-securing all deadbolts and gates, and uploading your digital photo report.

Standard Scope

What We Look For
During Every Visit.

Our visual checks follow a defined scope designed to cover the most common areas of vulnerability in unoccupied second homes.

Visual checks under kitchen and bathroom sinks, around water heaters, washing machines, and ceilings for discoloration, staining, or musty odors.

Verifying thermostat readouts, confirming air is circulating, and noting any uncharacteristic indoor humidity levels.

Ground-level visual observation of gutters, downspout outlets, decks, fences, detached garages, and pool water clarity.

Transparent Communication

What Happens If We Observe Something?

Clear, immediate communication without exaggeration.

Immediate Photo Notification

If our inspector notes an abnormal condition—such as a tripped breaker, leaking outdoor spigot, or storm debris—you receive a direct text or email notice with clear photos and description.

Owner Direction & Authorization

You remain in full control. You decide whether to contact your preferred local plumber, HVAC contractor, or landscaper.

Vendor Access Coordination

Upon request, we can meet your approved vendor on site, unlock the property, remain present for the visual duration, and secure the home when they depart.
