Your Home Doesn't Have to Be Occupied to Be Looked After.

Vacant and seasonal properties face unique challenges when left unattended for weeks or months at a time. A regular visual check helps identify visible warning signs before they become major surprises.

Visible Water Indicators

Checking under sinks, water heater pans, and ceilings for obvious moisture signs.

Severe Weather Effects

Observing exterior walls, rooflines, screens, and yards after heavy storms.

Mail Accumulation

Clearing full boxes, flyers, and visible packages from doorsteps.

HVAC Status Review

Confirming thermostat settings and basic air handler operation visually.

Who Chooses Homehaven Watch?

Fictional scenarios demonstrating how scheduled checks fit different ownership situations.

Seasonal Homeowners

Wintering Elsewhere

Homeowners who spend several months away during cold or hot seasons need regular confirmation that heating, plumbing, and perimeter security remain undisturbed.

Typical Cadence: Weekly or Bi-Weekly checks with post-freeze visual walkthroughs.
Vacation Homeowners

Weekend Getaways

Owners who use their property every few weeks benefit from mail retrieval, patio prep before arrival, and storm checks when severe weather passes through.

Typical Cadence: Bi-Weekly checks with storm preparation add-on.
Out-of-State Owners

Remote Properties

Owners who live states away and cannot easily drive over rely on trusted local visual eyes to coordinate contractor entry and report observed property conditions.

Typical Cadence: Complete Watch tier with vendor access assistance.
