What We Check

Key Focus Areas After Severe Weather

Structured visual observation as soon as municipal roads and weather permit safe transit.

Fallen Debris & Access

Observing blocked driveways, downed tree limbs, fence breaks, or compromised access paths.

Ground-Level Roofline

Visual scanning of visible shingles, gutters, soffits, and flashing from ground vantage points.

Exterior Standing Water

Checking for pooling near foundations, flooded patio drains, or overflowing pool water levels.

Utility Power Status

Verifying basic property power restoration, outdoor AC condenser condition, and thermostat status.

Rapid Notification

Immediate Digital Photo Dispatch

Within hours of completing the post-storm inspection, you receive an extensive visual report highlighting the condition of all perimeters.

If Visible Damage Is Observed:

We capture detailed close-up photos, note the exact location, provide you with immediate notification, and can coordinate entry for your preferred licensed roofing or cleanup contractors.

Service Scope & Legal Disclaimer

Post-storm checks consist of non-invasive, ground-level visual observations. Homehaven Watch does not provide licensed insurance claim adjusting, structural damage certification, professional roofing diagnostics, or hazardous debris removal. We coordinate with qualified licensed specialists on your behalf when requested.
