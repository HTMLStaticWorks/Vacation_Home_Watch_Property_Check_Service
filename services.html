Core Service

Vacant Home Checks

Comprehensive recurring walkthroughs of unoccupied residences to observe visible exterior, interior, plumbing, and climate conditions.

What's Checked: Entry doors, windows, under-sink piping, thermostats.
Recommended Cadence: Weekly or Bi-Weekly during off-season.

Severe Storm Preparation

Securing lightweight outdoor furniture, latching storm shutters, checking outdoor drains, and logging pre-storm condition photos.

What's Checked: Decks, patio items, storm shutters, exterior gates.
Recommended Cadence: Prior to forecasted high-wind or freeze events.

Post-Storm Visual Checks

Rapid site visits after severe weather events to document visible exterior impacts, fallen trees, standing water, and roofline debris.

What's Checked: Ground-level roofline, perimeter fences, water intrusion.
Recommended Cadence: Immediately following all regional severe storms.

Exterior & Perimeter Checks

Complete ground-level perimeter visual walk covering doors, windows, foundation lines, outdoor hose bibbs, and pool water levels.

What's Checked: Windows, siding, outdoor lighting, detached garages.
Recommended Cadence: Integrated into every scheduled visit.

Interior Systems Review

Observation of key living zones, testing faucets for flow (if requested), checking under sinks for leaks, and logging thermostat settings.

Scope of Services & Important Limitations

All Homehaven Watch services consist of non-invasive, visual inspections and concierge property oversight. We do not provide licensed technical diagnostics, structural certifications, legal tenant management, or active security guard details. We gladly coordinate with your licensed specialists for technical repairs.
