Proactive Care

Why Scheduled Checks Matter

Small, unnoticed occurrences can develop into major challenges over weeks of vacancy.

Plumbing & Supply Lines

Supply lines under sinks or toilet fill valves can seep slowly. Visual checks identify moisture before standing water spreads.

Climate Control Stability

Power surges or tripped breakers can disable air conditioning or heating, causing interior temperature spikes or freezing risks.

Perimeter Integrity

Confirming that all exterior doors, deadbolts, windows, and perimeter gates remain securely fastened and untampered.

Defined Scope

Our Comprehensive Walkthrough Protocol

During each scheduled visit, our inspector executes a systematic visual checklist covering both exterior grounds and interior living zones.

Exterior Ground-Level Review

Roofline visual check from ground, siding condition, window glass integrity, outdoor spigots, and mail/package collection.

Interior Room-by-Room Walkthrough

Inspecting ceilings for discoloration, running faucets (if requested), verifying refrigerator power, and logging thermostat settings.

Utility & Breaker Panel Visual Check

Observing breaker panel for tripped switches, water heater pan inspection, and noting any unusual electrical or mechanical hums.
