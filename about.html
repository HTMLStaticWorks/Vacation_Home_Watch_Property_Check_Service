Our Story & Standards

About Homehaven Watch

We exist to give seasonal, vacation, and second-home owners genuine confidence while they are away through attentive, structured visual property care.

OUR PURPOSE

Peace of Mind for the Home You Don't Visit Every Day.

Second homes are places of restoration, family memories, and personal investment. Leaving them unoccupied for weeks or months can create persistent worry.

Homehaven Watch was established with a singular focus: to be the dependable, local eyes and ears for homeowners who cannot be on site. We provide structured, non-invasive visual walkthroughs, clear photographic records, and calm, honest communication.
