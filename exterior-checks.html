Exterior Checklist

What We Check Outside Your Home

Careful visual observations during every scheduled perimeter sweep.

Doors, Windows & Screens

Testing all exterior deadbolts, sliding glass doors, window locks, and screen integrity for tampering or weather damage.

Pool & Spa Visual Check

Observing pool water levels, basic pump equipment sounds, surface clarity, and verifying security fence latching.

Lighting & Security Fixtures

Reviewing photocell lights, landscape pathway lighting, motion sensors, and security camera lenses for visible debris.

Landscaping & Drainage

Noting broken irrigation heads, washouts, overgrown limbs near rooflines, and standing water accumulation.

HVAC Condensers & Meters

Visual observation of outside compressor units, electric meter rotation, and propane tank gauge levels.

Pest & Wasp Indicators

Checking eaves and overhangs for visible wasp nests, termite mud tubes, or rodent burrows near foundation walls.

Visual Observation Notice

Exterior checks are strictly ground-level visual observations. Homehaven Watch does not climb roofs, disassemble pool pumps, perform certified pest treatments, or evaluate structural foundation engineering.
