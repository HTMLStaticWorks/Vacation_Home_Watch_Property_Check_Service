How Our Service Works

From our initial property walk-through to digital visit reports delivered directly to you. Here is how Homehaven Watch delivers reliable peace of mind while you are away.

STAGE 01

Property Information & Onboarding

We start by learning the details of your property, your travel patterns, key contact numbers, and any specific areas you want our specialist to monitor.

  • Initial property walk-through with owner or remote digital intake
  • Identification of main water shutoff, electrical panels, and HVAC units
  • Key custody protocol established with secure serial tagging
STAGE 02

Select Your Visit Schedule

Choose a recurring cadence that fits your departure schedule—weekly, bi-weekly, or customized seasonal cycles.

  • Weekly: Ideal during deep winter freeze or peak hurricane seasons
  • Bi-Weekly: Most popular for seasonal homeowners away 3–6 months
  • Monthly / On-Demand: Occasional checks for frequent travelers
Flexible Scheduling

You can pause, adjust, or increase visit frequency at any time as your travel plans change. No restrictive lock-in contracts.

Special Add-Ons: Pre-storm prep, post-storm checks, mail gathering, and vendor access.
STAGE 03

We Complete the Property Check

Our property care specialist arrives on schedule and conducts a systematic 24-point visual check.

  • 360-degree perimeter walk checking doors, windows, and outdoor fixtures
  • Interior walkthrough checking under sinks, ceilings, and HVAC settings
  • Mail and package retrieval placed securely inside the residence
STAGE 04

You Receive a Digital Visit Report

Immediately following the walkthrough, you receive a timestamped photo log with checklist results and notes.

  • GPS-verified time and date stamps
  • Clear photos of key areas and thermostat readouts
  • Direct alert if any unusual condition is observed
