Storm Preparation & Readiness Protocol

Essential pre-storm and post-storm guidance for vacation and second homes: securing outdoor accessories, shutter checks, drainage clearing, and post-weather observation.

Proactive Preparation Before Storms

When severe storms or tropical depressions are forecasted in vacation communities, proactive visual preparation helps reduce perimeter damage risks.

1. Securing Outdoor Hazards

High-velocity winds easily dislodge outdoor items. Store or tie down:

  • Patio umbrellas, dining sets, lounge cushions, and fire pit covers.
  • Hanging flower baskets, bird feeders, and decorative lawn fixtures.
  • Waste receptacles and pool cleaning poles.

2. Windows, Doors & Shutter Inspection

Confirm that sliding glass doors are fully seated in tracks, auxiliary pin locks are engaged, and accordion or roll-down storm shutters are latched securely.

3. Post-Storm Visual Verification

As soon as municipal road conditions are declared safe following a storm, our team conducts a ground-level visual sweep to document roofline conditions, standing water, fallen trees, and utility power status.

Emergency Authority Notice

Homehaven Watch provides non-invasive property checks and basic pre-storm item stowing prior to storm arrival. We do not provide structural boarding during active hurricanes or enter unsafe disaster zones.

Important: Always heed local emergency management evacuation directives and follow guidance from municipal authorities.
