7 Visible Signs Vacation Homeowners Should Check Before Departing

Marcus Vance
Property Care Specialist

Closing up a vacation home for several weeks or months requires more than simply turning the key in the front door deadbolt. Subtle visual indicators—if caught early—can prevent catastrophic water damage, power failures, or security oversights while you are miles away.

1. Braided Supply Line P-Traps and Shutoff Valves

Flexible stainless-steel braided lines under bathroom sinks, toilets, and refrigerators can degrade with age. Before leaving, inspect these lines with a flashlight for tiny beads of moisture, mineral crumbing, or rust around the crimped fittings.

Pro-Tip: If your home will be unoccupied for more than three weeks, shutting off the main water valve at the meter or interior manifold eliminates 90% of pressurized flood risks.

2. Refrigerator Ice Maker Moisture Tray

Ice makers that continue running in an empty house can jam or overflow the ice reservoir. Turn off the ice maker wire arm or toggle switch, empty the bin, and wipe the freezer dry.

3. Secondary Sliding Door Pin Latches

Standard patio sliding door handle latches can sometimes lift with vibration during heavy seasonal winds. Installing a secondary track rod or auxiliary deadbolt pin ensures the slider cannot be jarred open.

4. Thermostat Mode & Eco Setpoints

Verify that your thermostat is set to permanent 'Hold' or 'Vacation Eco Mode' rather than an automated daily schedule that might turn off systems during cold nights.

5. Unplugging Countertop Small Appliances

Toasters, coffee makers, and smart speakers draw phantom loads and can be vulnerable to seasonal lightning surges. Unplug them completely.

6. Mailbox & Delivery Porch Clearance

An overflowing mailbox or stacked packages on the front porch are the most visible indicators of an absent homeowner. Ensure mail is held or scheduled for local collection.

7. Scheduling Recurring Visual Property Checks

Even the most thorough departure checklist cannot account for events that happen weeks after you leave. Having a professional property watch specialist visit on a scheduled weekly or bi-weekly cadence provides ongoing verification.
