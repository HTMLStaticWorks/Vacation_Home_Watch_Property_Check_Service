Seasonal Home Preparation Guide

Best practices for closing and opening your seasonal property: winterization protocols, summer humidity management, outdoor furniture care, and vendor coordination.

Closing Your Home for the Season

Whether departing for a sunny winter or heading back to your primary residence after summer, a structured seasonal closing protocol prevents common vacant-home complications.

1. Winterization & Freeze Precautions

In cold climates, frozen pipes represent the single highest property hazard. To reduce risk before winter departure:

  • Keep heating set to at least 55°F (13°C) continuously; never shut heating systems off entirely.
  • Disconnect all outdoor garden hoses from exterior spigots to allow internal frost-free valves to drain.
  • Open cabinet doors beneath sinks on exterior walls to permit warm room air circulation.

2. Summerization & Humidity Control

In warm, coastal, or southern regions, elevated indoor humidity during unoccupied summer months can foster musty odors and condensation.

  • Set air conditioning to a stable range (76°F–78°F) with humidity targets below 55%.
  • Replace air return filters immediately prior to departure to prevent system strain.
  • Ensure interior doors between rooms remain open to maintain balanced return airflow.

3. Outdoor Item & Patio Management

High seasonal winds can turn lightweight patio furniture into projectiles. Store cushions, umbrellas, and dining chairs in garages or secured storage lockers.

4. Vendor Access & Ongoing Maintenance

Maintain your scheduled pool cleaner, landscaper, and pest control services while away. Homehaven Watch can provide key coordination to grant scheduled access and verify completion visually.

Safety & Qualified Trades Notice

This guide provides general property stewardship principles. It does not replace licensed HVAC, plumbing, or electrical engineering advice. Consult with qualified, licensed trades for professional winterization blow-outs or major mechanical servicing.
