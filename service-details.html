Why This Service Matters

When fine properties sit unoccupied for extended intervals, subtle environmental conditions can shift unnoticed. Unattended water leaks, failed HVAC compressors, storm debris, or mail pileup require a dependable local specialist to perform systematic visual checks.

What Is Included in Every Visit

Exterior & Perimeter

Door alignment, deadbolt testing, window screens, siding observation, and mailbox collection.

Interior & Systems

Under-sink moisture observation, ceiling stain checks, refrigerator verification, and thermostat readouts.

Visit Process & Documentation

Our specialists follow a structured digital checklist on mobile devices. Upon departure, GPS-verified photographs and observation notes are instantly compiled into your client visit log and emailed directly to you.

Service Scope Limitations

This service represents visual, non-invasive observation. We do not provide licensed trade diagnostics, engineering certifications, structural repairs, or legal security guard protection.
